abstract: "Let $\\mathcal A$ the affine algebra given by the ring $\\mathbb{F}_q[X_1,X_2,\\ldots,X_\\ell]/ I$, where $I$ is the ideal $\\langle t_1(X_1), t_2(X_2), \\ldots, t_\\ell(X_\\ell) \\rangle$ with each $t_i(X_i)$, $1\\leq i\\leq \\ell$, being a square-free polynomial over $\\mathbb{F}_q$. This paper studies the $k$-Galois hulls of $\\lambda$-constacyclic codes over $\\mathcal A$ regarding their idempotent generators. For this, first, we define the $k$-Galois inner product over $\\mathcal A$ and find the form of the generators of the $k$-Galois dual and the $k$-Galois hull of a $\\lambda$-constacyclic code over $\\mathcal A$. Then, we derive a formula for the $k$-Galois hull dimension of a $\\lambda$-constacyclic code. Further, we provide a condition for a $\\lambda$-constacyclic code to be $k$-Galois LCD. Finally, we give some examples of the use of these codes in constructing entanglement-assisted quantum error-correcting codes."
