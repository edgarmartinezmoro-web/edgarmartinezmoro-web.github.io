abstract: "The purpose of this work is to show how linear error-correcting codes can be understood by means of appropriate commutative semigroups with given generators. Given an error-correcting code $\\mathcal C$ we can define a binomial ideal $I(\\mathcal C)$ associated to $\\mathcal C$, therefore it seems natural to ask whether the above ideal match a semigroup ideal $I(S)$ given by some specific semigroup $S$. This construction establishes a strong relation between codes and semigroups and constitutes a means to apply numerous results in the field of semigroups to problems in information theory. Thus, some problems of Coding Theory could be addressed using techniques inspired by the theory of toric varieties."
