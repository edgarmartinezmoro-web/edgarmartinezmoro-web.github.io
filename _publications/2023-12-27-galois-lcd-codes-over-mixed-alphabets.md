abstract: "We study (Galois) linear complementary dual codes over mixed alphabets arising from finite chain rings. We give a characterization of when a given code is of this type and when it is Galois invariant. Finally, this leads to a study of the Gray image of $\\mathbb{F}_p\\mathbb{F}_p[\\theta]$-linear codes, where $p\\in\\{2, 3\\}$ and $\\theta\\neq\\theta^2=0$, that provides $\\mathbb{F}_p$-linear complementary dual codes."
