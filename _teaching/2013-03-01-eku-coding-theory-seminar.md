A series of colloquia, seminars and a working seminar on coding theory and related topics at the Department of Mathematics and Statistics of Eastern Kentucky University, during the spring semester of 2013, while I held the Vernon Wilson Endowed Chair at EKU. The seminar was organized with Steve Szabo.

The seminar page keeps the programme and the slides of the sessions, colloquia and undergraduate talks.
