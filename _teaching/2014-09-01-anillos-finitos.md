Course at the Escuela Venezolana de Matemáticas and the Escuela Matemática de América Latina y el Caribe (EMALCA), Mérida, Venezuela, 1–5 September 2014.

Teaching team: Edgar Martínez-Moro (IMUVa), Alejandro Piñera-Nicolás (IMUVa) and Ignacio F. Rúa (Universidad de Oviedo).

The main goal of the course is to train the students in the basic constructions with finite rings. Units: finite fields, Galois rings, finite local rings, error-correcting codes, and SAGE labs. Some abstract algebra (basic group and ring theory) and linear algebra is desirable but not essential.

The course text is the book *Introducción a los anillos finitos y sus aplicaciones* (in Spanish), written for the school.
