Science research project for high-school students run by IES Diego de Praves and the University of Valladolid, October 2026 – February 2027 (in Spanish).

The material contains the project proposal, the calendar, six one-hour sessions and a final 90-minute session. Topics: counting Latin squares and Sudoku grids, finite fields, error-correcting codes, the link between solving a Sudoku and correcting errors, cryptography, pseudorandom generators, and the theoretical limits of Sudoku puzzles. Each session has a timed outline, worked examples, proofs, exercises with short solutions and its own bibliography.

*Sudokus, juegos combinatorios y transmisión de la información: una introducción a la corrección de errores en información digital. Guiones y notas de las sesiones*, Zenodo, 2026. DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23147370](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23147370). License: CC BY 4.0.
